## Variation 2: The "Did You Know" Hook

**Did you know your brain is nearly 60% fat?**

And a significant portion of that fat is DHA — an omega-3 fatty acid most people don't get enough of.

Here's why that matters:

**For your brain:** DHA supports memory, learning, and mental clarity. Low levels are associated with cognitive decline and higher rates of depression.

**For your heart:** EPA and DHA reduce blood pressure, lower triglycerides, and decrease the risk of irregular heartbeats. The evidence is strong enough that cardiologists routinely recommend them.

**For your body:** Omega-3s help manage inflammation — not the kind you see, but the chronic, low-grade kind linked to diabetes, autoimmune disorders, and even cancer.

**For your mood:** Research consistently connects omega-3 intake with lower rates of anxiety and depressive symptoms.

We spend so much time optimizing productivity tools, workflows, and routines.

But optimizing the organ that runs everything? That deserves attention too.

If you're not supplementing omega-3s, it might be the easiest upgrade you make this year.

## Variation 3: The Contrarian Take

**Hot take: Most supplements are unnecessary. Omega-3s are the exception.**

I'm not a "take 15 pills every morning" person. Most of the supplement industry sells hope in a bottle.

But omega-3s are different. Here's why:

**The gap is real.**
Western diets are loaded with omega-6 fatty acids and dangerously low in omega-3s. The ideal ratio is around 4:1. Most people are closer to 20:1. That imbalance fuels inflammation.

**The research is deep.**
We're not talking about one flashy study. Thousands of peer-reviewed papers link omega-3 supplementation to better heart health, reduced inflammation, improved mental health, and stronger cognitive function.

**The risk is almost zero.**
Side effects are minimal. Drug interactions are rare. The cost is low. The upside is significant.

**The ROI compounds.**
You won't feel a dramatic shift on day one. But over months and years, adequate omega-3 intake protects your heart, brain, and joints in ways that add up.
