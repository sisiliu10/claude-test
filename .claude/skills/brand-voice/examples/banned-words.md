# Banned Words & Phrases

Never use any of these in any content: 

- unlock
- leverage
- game-changer
- synergy
- deep dive 
- it's giving
- at the end of the day
- in today's fast-paced world
- revolutionise / revolutionize 
- empower
- disrupt
- ecosystem (unless literally about nature)
- thought leader
- hack (as in "life hack)
- seamless
- robust
- cutting-edge
