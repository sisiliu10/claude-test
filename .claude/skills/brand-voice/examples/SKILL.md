# Brand Voice

## How I Sound
- Talk like you're chatting to one person over coffee, not presenting to a boardroom
- Use "you" and "I", never "we" or "our team"
- Short sentences. One idea per sentence. Punch, don't waffle.
- OK to start sentences with "And", "But", "So"
- Confident but not arrogrant. Helpful but not preachy.

## Words I NEVER Use
- See banned-words.md for a full list of words and phrases to avoid

## Platform-Specific Rules 

### LinkedIn
- Hook like first, then line break, then body
- End with a question or CTA
- No hashtags in the body - max 3 at the very end if any
- Under 200 words 

### X (Twitter)
- No hashtags ever
- Threads max 5 posts 
- Conversational, not performative
- Swearing OK occasionally
